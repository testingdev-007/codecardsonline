📖 Quick Start Rules: STACK OVERFLOW

A fast, tactical, educational card duel where you assemble software while defending against and deploying glitches!

1. Core End Condition

• The game ends immediately when ANY player runs out of cards in their hand! Whoever has the highest compiled score in their tabletop Stack at that moment wins.

2. Hand vs. Stack

• Your Hand (5 Cards): Private RAM. Cards in hand score 0 points.
• Your Stack: The public tabletop column. Only cards deployed here count toward final victory!

3. Stealing & Trading Clarifications

• Copy & Paste: YOU choose the exact Code Card (+) you want from the opponent's stack and snatch it.
• Team Brainstorm: Each player secretly selects 1 card from their own hand and swaps it with the opponent.
• Reboot: Discards the TOP-most card of the chosen stack (LIFO rule).

4. Strategic Reactions (Undo!)

Holding an Undo! (Ctrl + Z) prompts you with a choice whenever the AI attacks you: cancel it or save it!

🎓 The 31 CS Concepts Compendium

🟢 Code Cards (+1 to +5 Points)

Variables (+1): Storage for numbers, text, flags, and contact lists.
Loops (+2): Automatic repeaters (For, While, Do-While, Auto-Retry).
Decisions (+3): Smart conditional rules (If-Then, If-Else, Switch/Case).
Modular Architecture (+4): Reusable Functions, App Templates, and Cloud Links.
Optimized Algorithms (+5): Fast Search (High-Low halving) and Sorting.

🔴 Glitches (-1 to -4 Penalty Points)

Typo! (-1), Infinite Loop (-2), Frozen Screen (-3), Total Crash (-4).

⚡ Commands & ★ Game-Changers

Undo! (Ctrl+Z): Instant interrupt.
It's a Feature, Not a Bug!: Glitches become positive points!
Overclock!: Doubles your code score!
